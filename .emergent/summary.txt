<product_requirements>
The user requested a complete pharmacy management system with the following detailed characteristics:
*   **Login and Users**: Secure login with 5 pre-defined users (1 Admin, 4 Collaborators with specific access). Login must use a database (no localStorage) with encrypted passwords and separate admin/collaborator panels.
*   **Dashboard**: Display sales reports by selected dates, least-sold products, near-expiry product alerts (3 months), overdue client debts, and monthly/annual profit reports.
*   **PDV (Point of Sale)**: Fast search by barcode, multiple payment methods (cash, Pix, debit, credit, debt), sales linked to client debt control, and partial payment options for debts.
*   **Product Registration**: Fields for name, barcode, validity, price, quantity, physical location. Products listed alphabetically.
*   **Clients**: Registration with CPF and phone, debt control with color-coded alerts and automatic WhatsApp messages.
*   **Product Transfer**: Movement of products between different pharmacy units.
*   **Multi-unit Management**: Each pharmacy has its own database, with a Master Administrator view consolidating all data. Units must be configurable in the database.
*   **Extra Functionalities**: Electronic Fiscal Note (NFe) integration, Promotions and Discounts applied at PDV, Smart Stock alerts (low stock or near expiry), Pharmaceutical Module for controlled prescriptions, Advanced Financial Module (accounts payable/receivable, cash flow), and Employee Control (shift and permissions).
*   **Database**: A real MongoDB database, not localStorage.

**Current Implementation**: Login with professional branding, interactive Dashboard (shows real-time sales, low stock, pending debts with interactive modals), PDV (supports barcode and name search with suggestions), Products (CRUD operations,  field, editing functionality), Clients (CRUD operations, editing functionality), Promotions, and Advanced Reports.
